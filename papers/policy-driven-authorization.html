Policy-Driven Authorization Architectures For Modern Financial Regulatory Platforms

Abhiram Potharaju

Journal of International Crisis and Risk Communication Research · 9(4), 36–44 · 2026-04-02

Journal publication · 2026Original published work

Research overview

This paper examines a shift from traditional role-based access control toward policy-driven, context-aware authorization for financial regulatory platforms. It describes how attribute-based decisions can incorporate information about the user, device, transaction and regulatory context; how centralized policy decisions can work with distributed enforcement and caching; and how microservices and containerized infrastructure can support scalable policy updates and auditability. The discussion also addresses security constraints, least-privilege access and performance trade-offs.
