Human-Guided Artificial Intelligence for Adaptive Compliance Automation in Financial Institutions

Abhiram Potharaju

Journal of Computational Analysis and Applications · 35(4), 57–68 · 2026-04-06

Journal publication · 2026Original published work

Research overview

This paper presents a human-guided approach to artificial intelligence in financial compliance operations. The proposed architecture connects event-driven microservices, decision-confidence measures, and human review so that regulatory cases can be routed according to complexity and model certainty. It discusses automated processing for lower-risk cases, human validation and overrides for cases requiring interpretation, and feedback loops that improve model calibration and operational accountability. The work also considers interpretability, auditability, containerized deployment, and the trade-offs between processing scale and the need for expert judgment.
